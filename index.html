The Garage

Helpful guidance before you buy, service or personalise your vehicle.

01

Before you buy

Before committing to a purchase, share the make, model, year, budget and any non-negotiables. We can help you decide what should be checked before the deal is finalised, from condition and history to likely running costs and inspection priorities.

Live Chat
02

Before a service visit

When a problem appears, the timing and pattern matter. Note when the issue happens, what you noticed, any recent repairs and whether it affects safety or drivability. A short photo or video can be helpful too, and it often speeds up the first review.

Live Chat
03

Before you modify

When you are considering a change in finish, styling or accessories, tell us the vehicle and the outcome you want. We can help talk through practical options, compatibility and the likely cost before any work is agreed.

Good to know.

Quick answers before you make an enquiry.

How does repair coordination work?

Send your vehicle details and describe the issue. We’ll review it, discuss the next step and share the proposed work and quote before you approve.

Can you help source a specific car?

Yes. Share the make, model, year and budget you have in mind so we can review the request and discuss available options.

Can you help find spare parts?

Share your vehicle details and the part name, number or a photo. We’ll review sourcing options and confirm price and timing.

Do I approve the cost before work starts?

Yes. You review the proposed scope and customer-facing costs before you decide whether to proceed.
