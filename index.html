Good to know.

Quick answers before you make an enquiry.

How does repair coordination work?

Send your vehicle details and describe the issue. We’ll review it, discuss the next step and share the proposed work and quote before you approve.

Can you help source a specific car?

Yes. Share the make, model, year and budget you have in mind so we can review the request and discuss available options.

Can you help find spare parts?

Share your vehicle details and the part name, number or a photo. We’ll review sourcing options and confirm price and timing.

Do I approve the cost before work starts?

Yes. You review the proposed scope and customer-facing costs before you decide whether to proceed.
