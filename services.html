TINT & GLASS
PROTECTION.

Tint percentage refers to visible light transmission (VLT): the share of visible light that passes through the film. A lower VLT percentage means a darker tint; a higher percentage lets more light through. Options and suitable shades depend on your vehicle and applicable local requirements.

Understanding tint percentages

50% VLTLighter shade · lets in more light
35% VLTLight-medium shade
25% VLTMedium shade
15% VLTDark shade · more privacy
5% VLTVery dark shade
1% VLTDarkest shade shown

No tint is the clear-glass baseline. VLT is the share of visible light that passes through: lower percentages look darker. The illustration is a visual guide; existing glass and local requirements can affect the final result.
