    BOOKING POLICY

    Booking, Cancellation & Refund Policy

    1. Booking Confirmation

    All booking requests are subject to review by Teo DaCarguy Car Services. Submitting a request does not guarantee acceptance. Customers will be notified of the outcome, and available appointment slots will be managed to prevent scheduling conflicts.

    2. Payment & Final Approval

    Once your booking request has been reviewed, you may be invited to make payment. Payment alone does not confirm your appointment. After your payment has been verified, Teo DaCarguy Car Services will conduct a final review and manually approve your booking. Your appointment is only confirmed when you receive an official booking approval notification. If we cannot approve your booking after payment, we will contact you to discuss rescheduling or an appropriate refund.

    3. Cancellations & Rescheduling

    Customers should contact us as early as possible to cancel or reschedule an appointment. All changes are subject to availability. Any applicable cancellation charges will be communicated in advance.

    4. Refunds

    Refund eligibility depends on the circumstances, the stage of the booking, and any work or costs already incurred and disclosed before payment. If we cannot provide a service you have paid for, we will offer rescheduling or an appropriate refund. Approved refunds will normally be processed within 7–14 business days, although payment providers may require additional time.

    5. Delays & Additional Costs

    Service completion times may change due to unforeseen issues or parts availability. Any additional work or charges will be communicated to you and require your agreement before we proceed.

    6. Customer Agreement

    Customers must read and accept this policy before proceeding to payment. By accepting, you acknowledge that your appointment is not confirmed until you receive official approval from Teo DaCarguy Car Services.

    For assistance, please contact us through the official contact details on our website. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under applicable Nigerian law.
