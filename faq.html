CLEAR ANSWERS, BEFORE YOU START

A few things
you might wonder.

Each vehicle and request is different. If you don’t see your question, tell Teo what you need.

Make an enquiry
How does the repair process work?

Send a request with the vehicle details and a description of the issue. Teo reviews it, coordinates an assessment where needed, and shares the proposed work and quote before you approve.

Can you pick up or deliver my car?

Pickup and delivery can be discussed with your request. Availability, location and any associated cost are confirmed before you decide.

Can I request a specific car?

Yes. Share the make, model, year and budget you have in mind. Sourcing and inspection options are discussed for that vehicle.

Can you source spare parts?

Share the vehicle details and the part name, number or a photo. Teo can review sourcing options and confirm price and timing before you decide.

How do I get a quote?

Submit a consultation request with as much detail as you can. If an inspection or more information is needed, Teo will contact you before preparing a quote.

Do I have to bring my car to you?

Describe where the vehicle is and what it needs. Teo can discuss the practical next step, including coordination or collection where available.

How will I receive updates?

Choose WhatsApp, phone or email in your request. Customer tracking will be available when the private request system is connected.

How long does a repair take?

Timing depends on the diagnosis, parts availability and work involved. An estimate is shared when the scope is clear and updated if circumstances change.

When do I approve the work?

You review the proposed scope and customer-facing costs first. Work should only proceed after you agree to the quote.

How do I pay?

Payment details and accepted methods are confirmed with the quote or invoice before you approve.

We help coordinate repairs, maintenance, parts and vehicle sourcing through one clear point of contact.
